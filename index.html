Growth strategy · US bank

A bank-owned network that connects startups with investors

The bank wanted to grow its deposits. We built a platform around a value proposition where everyone wins: startups want capital, investors want startups they can trust, and the bank wants deposits. Startups open a business account to join and get checked by the bank. Investors join free and see those vetted startups, ranked by how well each one fits what they look for.

Business transformation · Certification body

One clear path for every student, from payment to certificate

Student records sat in separate systems across many countries, and staff tracked progress by email. We mapped the full student journey and built one five-step pipeline connected to the learning platform, with a live dashboard for leadership. Staff now step in only when something looks off.
